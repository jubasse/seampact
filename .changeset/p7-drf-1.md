The new `seampact-drf` Python target generates an api's frozen dataclass types, DRF serializers, `APIView` declarations, URLconf and typed settings on Django 5.2 and 6.x, and the compatibility matrix now checks DRF, Django Ninja and Django by major (`djangorestframework@3`, `django-ninja@1`, `django@6`).
